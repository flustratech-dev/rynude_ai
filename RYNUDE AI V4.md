# RYNUDE AI V4

## AI RESEARCH & DOCUMENT OPERATING SYSTEM

### Vision

Rynude bukan sekadar AI Chat.

Rynude adalah AI Research & Document Operating System yang mampu bersaing secara nasional maupun internasional dengan fokus pada:

* General AI Assistant
* Academic AI Assistant
* Research AI Assistant
* Report Writing AI Assistant
* Long Context AI Assistant
* Document Intelligence Platform
* Knowledge Workspace

